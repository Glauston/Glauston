## Product principles

**Useful before impressive.**  
Technology must reduce work, errors and decision time.

**Secure by design.**  
Access control, traceability, privacy and resilience are product requirements.

**Simple for the user.**  
Complex operations should not create complex interfaces.

**Integrated by default.**  
APIs, data portability and interoperability are part of the architecture.

**Built for evidence.**  
Logs, approvals, history and auditability should exist from the beginning.

**AI with human accountability.**  
Automation should accelerate work while keeping critical business and financial decisions under responsible human control.

## How I work

I combine **business analysis, process design, product thinking and AI-assisted development** to move from an operational problem to a testable product quickly.

Typical cycle:

`Problem → Process → Requirements → Architecture → UX → Build → Test → Audit → Improve`

The objective is continuous evolution without losing previously validated requirements.
